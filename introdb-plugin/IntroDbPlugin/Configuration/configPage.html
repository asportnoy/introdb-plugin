Segments

Stored as Outro segments. When IntroDB knows of a post-credits scene, the credits segment ends where the scene starts, so skipping the credits never skips the scene.

Scan scope

Leave everything unselected to fetch for all TV and movie libraries. Otherwise only items in the selected libraries, or the selected shows and movies, are fetched.

Libraries

Shows and movies

Scanning behavior

Skips any item with segments from any provider (Intro Skipper, Segment Editor, …). Faster on large libraries, but IntroDB won't add, say, credits to an episode that another provider already gave an intro.
Fetches segments for an item without any as soon as it's added (after a short wait for metadata) or played, instead of waiting for the Media Segment Scan task. This runs every enabled segment provider for that item.

Not-found cache

Items IntroDB has nothing for are remembered for a day (two weeks when the community confirmed there is nothing to skip), so repeat scans don't ask again. Clear it to look everything up again on the next scan.

Remove IntroDB segments

Deletes segments created by IntroDB from a library, show or movie. Segments from other providers are kept. They come back on the next scan unless you also narrow the scan scope or turn segment types off.
